India grows some of the most fragrant spices and flavourful produce in the world. Skyrah Impex exists to carry that flavour — whole, ground, dried or brewed — to kitchens, cafés and food businesses wherever they are.

Who We Are

An import & export house.
Rooted in Pollachi.

Skyrah Impex Private Limited is based in Pollachi, at the foot of the Anamalai hills in Tamil Nadu. Led by our Director, Ramesh Balakrishnan, we supply whole spices, spice powders, dehydrated vegetables, fruit powders and herbal infusions — in retail packs for homes, and in bulk for businesses in India and abroad.

How We Prepare

Indian farms.
Careful hands.

Our spices, vegetables, fruits, leaves and flowers come from Indian farms. They are cleaned, dried and milled with care, then sealed in resealable packs that protect their colour, aroma and flavour — so what reaches you tastes the way it should.
