Privacy Policy

Skyrah Impex Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or place an order, and how we use it.

We collect information you provide directly — name, phone number, email address and delivery address — when you place an order or contact us. We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, device type) to improve the site.

  • We never sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Your data is used only to process orders, respond to enquiries, and improve our products and service.
  • Payment details are handled by our payment gateway partners and are never stored on our servers.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@skyrahimpex.com.

Delivery Policy

Orders are dispatched from Pollachi, Tamil Nadu, within 24–48 hours of confirmation.

  • Standard delivery across India typically takes 3–6 business days from dispatch.
  • Remote or non-serviceable pin codes may take longer; we'll notify you if this applies to your order.
  • A tracking link is shared via SMS/email/WhatsApp once your order ships.
  • Shipping charges, if any, are confirmed with you before the order is placed.
  • Bulk and export orders ship on the packing, freight and delivery terms agreed in your quote.

Return & Refund Policy

Because our products are food items, we handle returns a little differently to protect quality and hygiene.

  • Unopened, unused products may be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Opened packs are eligible for return or replacement only in case of a genuine quality issue (damaged, expired, or incorrect item) — contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.
  • Return shipping costs for quality-issue claims are covered by us; costs for change-of-mind returns are borne by the customer.

To start a return, email info@skyrahimpex.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 9566 348 350 with your order number.
